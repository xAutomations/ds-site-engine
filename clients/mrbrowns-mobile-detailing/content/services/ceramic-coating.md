overview:
  heading: What Is Ceramic Coating?
  body:
    - A ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds with your vehicle's paint and hardens into a protective layer. Once cured, it shrugs off UV fading, road grime, bugs, and bird droppings, and it makes every wash afterward faster because dirt has a harder time sticking.
    - The result is a deep gloss that lasts. Water beads and rolls off, the color stays rich, and the finish keeps that just-detailed look between washes.
